QUADmapper Quick Start

Put an image or video on a quad, warp it to fit a surface, and send it to the projector. About two minutes to read.

1. The window

QUADmapper opens with a quad already on the canvas, so you can start at once.

  • Canvas: the large area in the middle. You see and edit your quads here.
  • Quad List: every quad, with tick boxes to show, hide and lock it. Use + Add Quad, Duplicate and Delete here.
  • Properties: the settings of whatever is selected: corners, mesh, crop, colour, and the settings of a selected output.
  • Resources: a window with tabs. The Media tab holds your files. In Advanced mode it also has the Project, Scenes, Outputs and Preview tabs.
  • Menus: File, Edit, Window, Output, AI and Help. A status bar along the bottom of the window shows messages.

Simple and Advanced mode

The Simple and Advanced buttons in the menu bar switch modes. Simple mode shows only what you need for a first mapping, plus the Apply Test Grid and Send to Projector (F11) buttons. Advanced mode adds scenes, outputs and the other tabs. QUADmapper starts in the mode you used last. Your panel layout is remembered between runs; Window > Reset Layout restores the default.

2. Your first mapping

Load a picture or video, pull the quad's corners onto your surface, then send it to the projector.

Load media

  1. Open the Media tab. Click Load Files... for single files, or Load Folder... for a whole folder.
  2. Drag a thumbnail from the tab onto the quad on the canvas.

For a video you can also use the Video Player panel (open it from the Window menu): select the quad, then click Load Video....

Warp it

  1. Click a corner of the quad, or press Tab to select a corner. Shift+Tab goes the other way round.
  2. Press the arrow keys to nudge the corner. Hold Shift to move 10 times as far. Hold Alt to move a tenth as far, for fine adjustment.
  3. You can also drag a corner with the mouse.
  4. Repeat for each corner until the picture sits on your surface.
The arrow keys and Tab belong to the canvas while a quad is selected. Ctrl+Z undoes a move.
A quad whose lock tick box is on in the Quad List cannot be reshaped or deleted until you unlock it.

Send it to the projector

  1. Press F11, or click Send to Projector (F11) in Simple mode. The same command is in the menu as Output > Perform Mode.
  2. The picture goes fullscreen on the projector.
  3. Press Esc or F11 to come back to the editor.
To choose which screen is the projector, set up the output in Advanced mode first (see Outputs).

3. Finer warping

Four corners fix a flat surface. For a curved surface, turn the quad into a mesh and move its points.

  1. Select the quad and press Ctrl+M (Toggle Mesh Edit). The same switch is the Edit Mesh Vertices tick box in Properties.
  2. Use Tab, the arrow keys and the mouse as before, now on the mesh points.
  3. Press Alt+= for a finer mesh (Increase Mesh Resolution) and Alt+- for a coarser one (Decrease Mesh Resolution).
  4. Press Alt+R (Reset Mesh) to put the mesh back to the plain corner-pin shape.

4. Scenes and takes

A scene is one full arrangement of quads. A take puts a scene on air.

  1. In Advanced mode, use the Scenes tab to add, duplicate, rename and reorder scenes.
  2. Press 1 to 9 to put that scene on air. The keys work in the editor and on a focused projector window, whatever your keyboard layout.
  3. For a crossfade, set Crossfade (seconds) in Edit > Preferences... (General tab). 0 is a cut.

5. Outputs

An output is one projector, or one window on a screen. Work in Advanced mode.

  1. Open the Outputs tab in the Resources window.
  2. Click Add Output. Click Refresh Monitors if you plugged in a display after starting.
  3. Select the output. In Properties, choose its screen in the Monitor list, then set its resolution and rotation.
  4. In the Scenes tab, choose which output shows each scene.

An output can show slices of the scene, each with its own warp and edge blending where two projectors overlap. Select the output to edit them in Properties.

6. During the show

  • Blackout: Ctrl+B turns every projector black. Press it again to bring the picture back. It is also in the Output menu.
  • Perform Lock: Ctrl+Shift+L (or Output > Perform Lock) stops accidental edits. Locking is immediate. To unlock, QUADmapper asks Unlock the Show? and you confirm.

What the lock blocks

Editing quads on the canvas, the Edit commands (including undo and delete), dropping media, New and Open, importing 3D objects, calibration and slice editing, AI assist, MIDI learn and Preferences.

What keeps working

Scene takes and fades, blackout, F11 and Esc, OSC and MIDI control, the Video Player transport, saving, canvas zoom and pan, and Exit.

7. Saving, autosave and recovery

  • File > Save Project (Ctrl+S) saves. Save Project As... (Ctrl+Shift+S) saves under a new name. Projects are .qmap files. File > Open Recent lists the last ten.
  • QUADmapper also saves unsaved work to a recovery file, every minute by default. Change this in Edit > Preferences... under Auto-save.
  • After a crash, QUADmapper opens a Recover Unsaved Work window the next time. Click Restore to get the work back, or Discard to drop it.
  • If a project's media files have moved, use File > Relocate Missing Media.... Missing media shows black on the projectors.
  • Open projects only from people you trust. A project can point at media on a network share (\\computer\share\...), and opening it makes Windows connect to that computer and sign in with your Windows account.

9. Remote control by OSC and MIDI

A lighting desk, a tablet or a MIDI controller can take scenes, trigger blackout and move controls.

  1. Open Window > Control (OSC / MIDI).
  2. For OSC, tick Enable OSC input. QUADmapper listens on the UDP port shown there (8000 to begin with). Change the port, then click Apply. OSC is off until you turn it on.
  3. For MIDI, tick your device in the MIDI list, then use MIDI Learn to bind a control to a target.

For example, /qm/blackout toggles blackout, and /qm/scene/take takes a scene.

10. Where to get help

The Help menu has User Guide, AI Features Guide, Keyboard Shortcuts, Open Log Folder, Report a Bug, Open-Source Licences and About QUADmapper. If you report a bug, attach the log from Open Log Folder. The AI features are a beta, off until you tick Edit > Preferences... > General > AI Assist (beta); that adds the AI (Beta) menu and the AI Features Guide item. They have their own guide, ai-features-guide.html.
